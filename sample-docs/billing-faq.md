# Billing FAQ

## Plans
- **Free**: 5 GB storage, 1 device.
- **Pro**: $12 per user per month billed annually, or $15 billed monthly. 1 TB storage, unlimited devices.
- **Business**: $20 per user per month billed annually. 5 TB storage, admin console, SSO.

## Changing your plan
Go to **Account > Billing > Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades
take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Refunds
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you
can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid month. Refund requests are handled by the
billing team.

## Updating your payment method
Go to **Account > Billing > Payment method** and click **Update card**. We accept Visa, Mastercard,
American Express and PayPal.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the account owner on each billing date and are also available under
**Account > Billing > Invoices**, where they can be downloaded as PDF.
