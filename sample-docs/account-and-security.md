# Account and Security

## Resetting your password
1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your account email. A reset link valid for 60 minutes is sent to that address.
3. If the email doesn't arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.

## Enabling two-factor authentication (2FA)
Go to **Account > Security > Two-factor authentication**, click **Enable**, and scan the QR code with an
authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password. Save the 10 backup codes somewhere safe.

## Lost access to your 2FA device
Sign in with one of your backup codes. If you have no backup codes, account recovery must be done by the
support team after identity verification.

## Adding users (Business plan)
Admins can invite users from **Admin console > Users > Invite**. Invited users receive an email and count
toward your seat total once they accept.

## Deleting your account
Go to **Account > Settings > Delete account**. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period.
